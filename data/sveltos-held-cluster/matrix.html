Sveltos held cluster, the closing-audit matrix

Chapter six holds one cluster back on purpose. Two reviewed advances raise backgroundController.replicas from 2 to 3 and then from 3 to 4 on the shared base. Between them one production cluster, hx-sveltos-env-prod-a, is restored to its exact pre-advance revision through the same approval gate every forward change passes. The second advance upgrades that cluster's variant like every other, and then nobody approves it. The hold is the absence of that one approval. The closing audit reads the fleet at three points on purpose, and this matrix is that audit; its twin hx-sveltos-env-prod-b proves the fleet moved on around the held cluster. The observed columns come from the committed live receipt in runs/sveltos-held-cluster-proof/receipt.yaml.

Boundary: This chapter does not claim a fleet-wide undo, a failure budget, or an automated verification step between waves. It restores one target to an exact revision and keeps it there on purpose, with the fleet's mixed state recorded as facts rather than drift.
