- Its record, the Space chaos-management, holds every delivery profile; the root ClusterProfile chaos-management on the management cluster fetches the record's releases and applies them, taking over any object of the same kind and name already there. Removing a unit from the record and releasing withdraws what that unit delivered.
- Releasing the record needs a change order on the workflow chaos-management/record (one stage, record, in the Space itself) and the approver's approval. Nobody may publish it directly; the component refuses.

Always name the sandbox; never point it at a fleet cluster.
